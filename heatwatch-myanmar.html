Method

  • Data. NASA POWER daily point API (community=AG): T2M_MAX, T2M_MIN, RH2M, PRECTOTCORR, GWETROOT, 1981–2025, at each region's coordinates. Vegetation: MODIS NDVI monthly means (record starts 2000). This prototype currently runs on synthetic sample data with the same structure.
  • Threshold. For each calendar day, the 90th percentile of T2M_MAX over the 1981–2010 baseline, pooled across a ±7-day window (15 days × 30 years).
  • Heat wave. 3 or more consecutive days with T2M_MAX above that day's threshold. Days with T2M_MAX ≥ 40 °C are flagged separately as extreme-heat days.
  • Heat index. NWS Rothfusz regression from T2M_MAX and afternoon humidity. RH2M is a daily mean, so humidity at the time of Tmax is estimated by holding vapour pressure constant between the daily mean temperature ((T2M_MAX+T2M_MIN)/2) and Tmax.
  • Trend. Two-sided Mann-Kendall test (tie-corrected, no pre-whitening) on annual heat-wave days; magnitude from Sen's slope.
  • Earth-system chain. Mar–May means, standardised against 1981–2010 (NDVI against 2000–2010).
  • Farmer calendar. Uses an absolute threshold, not the percentile one: share of the last 30 years in which a week had 3 or more days with T2M_MAX ≥ 35 °C (the rice-flowering damage level). Low < 20 %, medium 20–50 %, high ≥ 50 %. A percentile-based heat wave is equally likely in every season by construction, so it cannot rank weeks of the year.
